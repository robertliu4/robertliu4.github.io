Interconnect / UI Board (v1.4)

The board that ties the robot's onboard computer to every signal around it — radio, motors, sensors, switches, and display.

Code ↗ Team ↗

Overview

This is UBC Thunderbots' user-interface and interconnect PCB. It sits with the robot's onboard computer (a Jetson Nano) and breaks its interfaces out to the rest of the robot — the wireless radio, the motor driver, the encoders and switches, and the display — conditioning and isolating those signals along the way so the compute talks cleanly to noisy, high-current hardware.

I worked on the v1.4 revision of the board.

Architecture

What's on the board

  • Compute interface — connects to the Jetson Nano and fans its I/O out to the robot.
  • Wireless radio — an RF module for comms with the field computer, driven over an SPI link.
  • Signal conditioning — buffers, low-pass filters (e.g., RC filtering on the encoder inputs), and optocouplers that isolate the low-voltage logic from the motor side.
  • Robot I/O — motor driver, interface switches, and a status display, plus on-board 5 V power.

My work on v1.4

  • Worked the v1.4 revision — layout and schematic changes, including cleaning up the radio connection and pruning unused signals and buffers from the previous revision.
