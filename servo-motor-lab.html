Servo Motor Characterization

Characterizing a servo motor's behavior through measurement and Python data analysis.

Role: Measurement & data analysis
Course: ENPH259 (Experimental Techniques), UBC — final lab

Overview

ENPH259 is UBC Engineering Physics' experimental techniques course, focused on careful measurement, instrumentation, and data analysis. For the final lab I characterized a servo motor — driving it, measuring its response, and working up the results in a Jupyter notebook.

What I did

  • Ran the servo motor experiment and collected the measurement data.
  • Analyzed and visualized the results in Python with NumPy, pandas, and Matplotlib.
