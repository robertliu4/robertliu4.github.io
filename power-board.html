Power Board (v3.6)

The robot's power board — I did much of the layout and am now improving its thermal and reliability performance.

Role: Layout · thermal & reliability — v3.6 revision
Team: UBC Thunderbots (RoboCup autonomous soccer robots)

Code ↗ Team ↗

Overview

The power board distributes and regulates power across the Thunderbots robot, feeding the compute, motors, and control electronics. Under sustained match load, a power board has to stay cool and stable — heat and marginal reliability are what take robots off the field.

I did a significant share of the board's layout, and I'm currently working on the v3.6 revision.

What I did

  • Completed a large portion of the board layout.
  • Currently diagnosing and resolving overheating hotspots and reliability issues to make the board robust under sustained load.
