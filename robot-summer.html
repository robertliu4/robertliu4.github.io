Robot Summer · Champion Robot

Two autonomous robots that cooperate in real time to assemble a burger. Built by a four-person team, they took first place, beating out every other team in UBC's Engineering Physics robotics competition.

Watch demo ↗

Overview

Robot Summer is UBC Engineering Physics' annual second-year robotics competition. Teams design, build, and program autonomous robots from scratch over the summer. Our challenge was cooperative: two robots working together in real time to place ingredients, "cook" patties, and assemble a burger, mimicking the teamwork of Overcooked. Everything ran autonomously, and the two robots had to stay coordinated with no human input.

I owned two sides of the build, the mechanical robot and the electrical system that ran it, and the robots took first place against 14 other teams.

Architecture

What I did

  • Mechanical design: designed the robot's mechanical structure, drivetrain, and the actuation (a stepper-driven carousel plus servos) that let it manipulate ingredients.
  • Custom PCBs: designed and built the boards in KiCad: power (with current sensing), sensor, actuator, I/O, and a distribution board with the stepper driver. The H-bridge motor drivers use opto-isolators to separate the high- and low-voltage grounds.
  • Bring-up & debug: verified signals between the ESP32 and the stepper, servo, and H-bridge drivers with AD2 oscilloscopes and logic analyzers.
