Robot Summer — Champion Robot

An autonomous competition robot built with a teammate, where I owned both the mechanical build and the full electrical system.

Role: Mechanical & Electrical Design
Team: Two-person team with Youssef Elbaz
Where: Vancouver, BC

Teammate's writeup ↗

Overview

Robot Summer is a build-from-scratch robotics competition where a small team designs, fabricates, and programs a fully autonomous robot over a summer. Our robot had to sense its surroundings and drive itself through the course without any human control, which meant the mechanical chassis, the electronics, and the firmware all had to work together reliably.

I took ownership of two sides of that: the physical robot and the electrical system that brought it to life.

What I did

  • Mechanical design — designed the robot's mechanical structure and the mounting/drivetrain that carried the electronics and actuators.
  • Custom PCBs — designed and built the boards in KiCad for battery management, signal routing, and power distribution.
  • Bring-up & debug — used AD2 oscilloscopes and logic analyzers to verify the signals between an ESP32 and the stepper, servo, and H-bridge motor controllers, tracking down issues across the power and signal paths.
